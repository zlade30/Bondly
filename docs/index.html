How it works

Every lesson takes just a few minutes

1

Read a tiny story

Each idea comes as a short card, like why “You never listen!” puts people on guard.

2

Practise it

Answer a quick quiz, fill in the blank, or reflect on your own relationships, with friendly feedback.

3

Try it today

Finish with one small, real-life step to try, like a kind, clear “no” or a two-minute repair.

Privacy

Your relationships stay yours

What you share with Bondly is personal, so it stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up.

  • No account, sign-up or email needed
  • Your answers, check-ins and journal are stored on your device
  • Optional sign-in backs them up privately, so only you can read them
  • No ads, no tracking and no analytics
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server

Read the full privacy policy

Bondly is for everyday relationship skills. It isn’t therapy, counselling or medical advice. If you are in danger or being hurt by someone, please contact your local emergency services or a support service in your area.
