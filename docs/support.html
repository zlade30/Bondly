Subscriptions and purchases

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers can try Bondly free for 7 days. You won’t be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. If you don’t cancel, your subscription starts at the end of the trial.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

iPhone: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Bondly. Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bondly. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription.

I paid but Bondly is still locked

On the plans screen, tap Restore. Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you used to buy. If that doesn’t work, email us with the date of the purchase.

I got a new phone or reinstalled Bondly

Tap Restore on the plans screen to get your purchase back. To bring back your progress and journal too, sign in to your Bondly account in Settings → Account (if you made one).

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. For the App Store, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, see Google Play’s refund page.

Your data

Where is my journal stored?

On your phone. If you sign in, it’s also backed up privately to your account so only you can read it. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account in Bondly. This permanently deletes your account and backup. You can also email us to ask for it.

Reminders

I’m not getting my daily reminder

Check that reminders are on in Bondly’s Settings, and that notifications for Bondly are allowed in your phone’s settings. Bondly skips the reminder on days you’ve already practised.

Need more help?
