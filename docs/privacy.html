Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version: Bondly works without an account and keeps everything you share on your phone. If you choose to sign in, your progress and journal are backed up to your private account so you can use them on another phone. Only you can read that backup. There are no ads, analytics or trackers, and we never sell or share your information.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Bondly mobile app (“Bondly”, “we”, “us”) and this website. Bondly is provided by Zal Studio. You can contact us at zal.studio.25@gmail.com.

2. What Bondly stores, and where

To personalise lessons and remember your progress, Bondly saves the following on your device, in the app’s private storage. If you sign in (see section 3), the same information is also backed up to your account:

InformationWhy it’s used
Your first name, the relationship you want to grow, your focus areas, your quiz answers, your daily goal and your first mood check-in (from onboarding)To greet you, choose which lessons come first and draw your connection map
Which lessons you’ve finished, and on which daysTo show your progress, journey map and streak
Your daily check-ins (a mood for each day)To show your check-in calendar and simple patterns
Your journal entries, including any mood and “who it was about” tag, and anything you write in a lesson’s writing promptSo you can read, edit and reflect on them later
Your reminder settings (on or off, and the time)To schedule your daily reminder

If you don’t sign in, none of this is sent to us or to anyone else, and the app doesn’t need the internet to work. Reminder settings always stay on your device.

3. Optional account and backup

Accounts are optional. You can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with a link or code sent to your email address. If you do:

  • Account details: we receive your email address and a user ID from the sign-in method you choose. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive Apple’s private relay address instead.
  • Your backup: your onboarding answers, lesson progress, check-ins and journal entries (the information in the table above, apart from reminder settings) are stored in your account so they stay in sync across your phones.
  • Where it’s stored: accounts and backups are hosted by Supabase, our cloud database provider, which processes them on our behalf. Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules let each signed-in person read and change only their own backup.
  • How it’s used: only to sign you in and keep your backup in sync. We don’t read your journal, use your information for advertising, or sell or share it.

Bondly doesn’t include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.

4. Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, Bondly asks your phone for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; they are not sent from a server. You can change the time or turn reminders off in Bondly’s Settings, or turn off notifications for Bondly in your phone’s settings at any time.

5. Permissions

The only permission Bondly asks for is notifications, and only if you choose to turn on reminders. Bondly doesn’t access your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

6. Device backups

If your phone backs up app data (for example with iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), your phone may include Bondly’s data in that backup. Those backups are handled by your device’s operating system and backup provider under their own privacy terms, not by us.

7. Deleting your information

  • Delete a single journal entry from the entry screen in the Reflect tab.
  • Clear everything on your phone with Settings → Start over → Replay onboarding. This permanently removes your answers, progress, check-ins and journal from the app and signs you out. Your account backup is kept until you delete your account.
  • Delete your account and its backup with Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes them from our provider straight away.
  • Deleting the Bondly app deletes all of its data from your device, but not your account. You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing zal.studio.25@gmail.com.

We can’t recover information once it has been deleted.

8. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit; see GitHub’s privacy statement for details. The site loads the Nunito font from Google Fonts, so Google may also receive your IP address when you open a page. The website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking.

9. Children

Bondly is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If Bondly changes how it handles information, we’ll update this policy and change the date above before that change takes effect. Your information will never leave your device unless you choose to sign in.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at zal.studio.25@gmail.com.
