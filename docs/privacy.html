Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version: Bondly keeps everything you share on your phone. We don’t have accounts, servers, ads, analytics or trackers, so we never receive your answers, check-ins or journal.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Bondly mobile app (“Bondly”, “we”, “us”) and this website. Bondly is provided by [your name or company]. You can contact us at [contact email].

2. What Bondly stores, and where

To personalise lessons and remember your progress, Bondly saves the following only on your device, in the app’s private storage:

InformationWhy it’s used
Your first name, the relationship you want to grow, your focus areas, your quiz answers, your daily goal and your first mood check-in (from onboarding)To greet you, choose which lessons come first and draw your connection map
Which lessons you’ve finished, and on which daysTo show your progress, journey map and streak
Your daily check-ins (a mood for each day)To show your check-in calendar and simple patterns
Your journal entries, including any mood and “who it was about” tag, and anything you write in a lesson’s writing promptSo you can read, edit and reflect on them later
Your reminder settings (on or off, and the time)To schedule your daily reminder

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not connect to the internet to work.

3. What we collect

Nothing. Bondly has no accounts and no sign-up, and it doesn’t include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools. We don’t sell, share or have access to your information, because it never reaches us.

4. Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, Bondly asks your phone for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; they are not sent from a server. You can change the time or turn reminders off in Bondly’s Settings, or turn off notifications for Bondly in your phone’s settings at any time.

5. Permissions

The only permission Bondly asks for is notifications, and only if you choose to turn on reminders. Bondly doesn’t access your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

6. Device backups

If your phone backs up app data (for example with iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), your phone may include Bondly’s data in that backup. Those backups are handled by your device’s operating system and backup provider under their own privacy terms, not by us.

7. Deleting your information

  • Delete a single journal entry from the entry screen in the Reflect tab.
  • Clear everything with Settings → Start over → Replay onboarding. This permanently removes your answers, progress, check-ins and journal from the app.
  • Deleting the Bondly app also deletes all of its data from your device.

Because we never receive your information, we can’t recover it for you once it’s deleted.

8. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit; see GitHub’s privacy statement for details. The site loads the Nunito font from Google Fonts, so Google may also receive your IP address when you open a page. The website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking.

9. Children

Bondly is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app stores everything on the device and sends nothing to us, we hold no information about any user, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If Bondly changes how it handles information (for example, by adding optional accounts or syncing between devices), we’ll update this policy and change the date above before that change takes effect, and we’ll ask you in the app before any of your information leaves your device.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at [contact email].
