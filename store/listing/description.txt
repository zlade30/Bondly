Grow closer to the people you love, 5 minutes a day.

Bondly turns relationship skills into bite-size lessons you can actually keep up with. Learn to speak openly, listen well, set kind boundaries and work through conflict, then try it with the people in your life the same day.

A JOURNEY FOR EVERY RELATIONSHIP
Pick up to three relationships to focus on, and Bondly builds your day around them:
• Partner: speak openly, handle disagreements and stay close
• Dating: be curious, send honest signals and keep comfortable boundaries
• Friendships: listen, show up and keep up as needs change
• Family: warmer, clearer conversations with parents, siblings and relatives
• Work: clear requests, feedback and respectful teamwork
• Yourself: notice how you show up, name your needs and respond more steadily

Every journey has 10 levels to grow through, one small step at a time.

PRACTISE REAL MOMENTS, NOT THEORY
Each lesson takes a few minutes and is made for real life:
• Quick questions with kind, instant feedback
• Everyday scenes where you choose what to say
• Matching, sorting and fill-in-the-blank activities
• Short reflections and a "Try it today" challenge to use straight away

SKILL REFRESHERS
Brush up on the skills that matter in every relationship: Communicate with Care, Listen to Understand, Build Trust, Set Boundaries, Resolve Conflict and Deepen Connection. Each has 10 levels of its own.

BUILD A HABIT THAT STICKS
• Choose a gentle daily goal: 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes
• Celebrate when you reach it, and keep your streak going
• Get one friendly reminder a day at a time that suits you, and none on days you've already practised

CHECK IN AND REFLECT
• Log how you feel each day in a few seconds
• Keep a private journal of moments, big or small
• Look back on your month in a calendar of check-ins

WATCH YOUR CONNECTION GROW
Your connection map shows how your communication, trust, boundaries, conflict and connection skills grow as you learn, alongside your streak and progress in each journey.
